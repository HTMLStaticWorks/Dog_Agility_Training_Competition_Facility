Member Etiquette

To maintain a safe and premium environment for all members, we require strict adherence to our facility rules.

  • Clean up after your dog immediately. Failure to do so may result in membership revocation.
  • Do not adjust contact equipment heights without staff permission.
  • Limit off-peak ring time to 60 minutes if others are waiting.
