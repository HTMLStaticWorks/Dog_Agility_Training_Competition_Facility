What to Bring to Class

  • High-value, soft treats (lots of them!)
  • A tug toy or your dog's favorite reward
  • 4-6 foot standard leash (no retractables)
  • Athletic shoes for running on turf
  • Water bowl and crate (optional)

Handler Fitness Recommendations

Sprint Intervals

Short burst sprints to match your dog's speed off the start line.

Core Stability

Essential for maintaining balance during front and blind crosses.

Dynamic Stretching

Prevent injuries by warming up before every class or trial run.
